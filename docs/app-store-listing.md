ĐẾM CỬ ĐỘNG THAI
• Đếm cử động chỉ bằng một chạm, ngay cả trên màn hình khoá và Dynamic Island.

ĐẾM CƠN GÒ

Luna Mom is with you from tracking your cycle and trying to conceive to the day your baby arrives — gentle, simple and private.

CYCLE TRACKING
• Log your period with one tap and see when the next one is expected.
• A clear monthly calendar: period days, predicted days and everything you logged. Edit your periods right on the calendar.
• Cycle history: average length, typical range and every past cycle. Add earlier periods for better predictions.
• Log flow, mood, symptoms and notes for any day.
• "Trying to conceive" mode: your likely fertile days, ovulation tests and basal body temperature.
• A daily birth control pill reminder (21 + 7 or 28-pill packs) — mark it "Taken" right from the notification.
• Takes the contraception you use into account. Luna Mom is not a form of contraception.

PREGNANCY
• One calm screen a day: your week, the days to go and an illustration of your baby for every week.
• How big is your baby: estimated weight from the Hadlock standard — the one ultrasound machines commonly use — and a fruit of about the same weight.
• Week-by-week articles for you and your baby, plus a trimester library with cited sources (WHO, ACOG, NHS, Vietnam Ministry of Health).
• Log symptoms, track your weight and antenatal appointments, with a reminder the day before.

KICK COUNTER
• Count movements with one tap, even from the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island.
• Daily and weekly history, and an optional daily reminder.

CONTRACTION TIMER
• Tap once when a contraction starts and again when it ends; Luna Mom records how long each one lasts and how far apart they are.
• Works right from the Lock Screen, without opening the app.
• Reminds you to contact your doctor or maternity unit when contractions become regular according to common guidance, or if they start before 37 weeks. Luna Mom does not diagnose labor.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no trackers.
• All your data stays on your iPhone. Back it up to a file to move to a new iPhone, and delete everything at any time.

Luna Mom is available in English and Vietnamese.

Note: Luna Mom provides information only and does not replace a diagnosis or your doctor's advice. Contact your doctor or maternity unit if you are worried, and call emergency services if you notice warning signs.

Privacy policy: https://lmtiep.github.io/kick-counter/privacy.html
